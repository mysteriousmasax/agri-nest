AGRI‑NEST Foundation

The AGRI‑NEST Foundation supports resilient smallholder agriculture across East Africa — research, grants, training, and community programs open to all AGRI‑NEST members.

Our Mission

Empower smallholder farmers with access to climate-smart practices, finance, and market linkages.

Programs

  • Field trials & agronomy support
  • Subsidised inputs for climate resilience
  • Market access & cooperative strengthening
